Recording: Open Record and tap the microphone. A red recording card, elapsed timer, waveform, system microphone indicator, and Live Activity remain visible while recording. Locking the phone or switching apps intentionally keeps the user-started recording active. Pause/Resume stops and restarts capture without finalizing the file. Stop & Save finalizes it on the device.

AI disclosure: Before the first transcription, readable-format, summary, or chat request, the app explains that content is sent to OpenAI and, for text features, OpenRouter/model providers. Declining leaves recording, recovery, library browsing, playback, export, and non-AI extraction available. New recordings and shared audio transcribe automatically only after this permission is given, and Settings > AI processing turns that off.

Share Extension: Sharing audio saves it on the device. If AI processing permission and automatic transcription are on, the upload starts in the background without opening the app, using a credential limited to uploading recordings. Otherwise the file waits in the app.

Account deletion: Settings > Danger zone > Delete account and data. The app confirms the destructive action and asks for the current password when one is set, purges account-owned application data, queues durable storage cleanup, removes device recordings owned by that account, and signs out.

AI safety report: Open an item with a generated summary or chat response and use Report summary or Report response. The report includes the selected output, category, and optional note; it does not attach another copy of the source media.
