Media Tools requires an account because recordings, transcripts, PDFs, chats, and collections are private, account-scoped workspace data.

Sign in: Media Tools has its own accounts and no third-party or social login. Open the app and sign in with an email and password on the first screen, use Create an account to register with an emailed code, or Forgot password to reset one. Continue with passkey and Use a recovery code are also available. App Review may use the non-expiring review account supplied in App Review Information.

Recording: Tap Record at the bottom of the Library. A red recording indicator, elapsed timer, waveform, system microphone indicator, and Live Activity remain visible while recording. Locking the phone or switching apps intentionally keeps the user-started recording active. Pause and Resume stop and restart capture without finalizing the file. Stop finalizes it on the device.

AI disclosure: Before the first transcription, readable-format, summary, or chat request, the app explains that content is sent to OpenAI and, for text features, OpenRouter/model providers. Declining leaves recording, recovery, library browsing, playback, export, and non-AI extraction available. New recordings and shared audio transcribe automatically only after this permission is given, and Settings (the gear on the Library screen) > Recording > Automatically transcribe turns that off.

Share Extension: Sharing audio saves it on the device. If AI processing permission and automatic transcription are on, the upload starts in the background without opening the app, using a credential limited to uploading recordings. Otherwise the file waits in the app.

Account deletion: Settings (the gear on the Library screen) > Delete account and data, at the end of the list. The app confirms the destructive action and asks for the current password when one is set, purges account-owned application data, queues durable storage cleanup, removes device recordings owned by that account, and signs out.

AI safety report: Open an item with a generated summary and choose Report Summary from the toolbar's More menu, or tap the More button under a chat response and choose Report Response. The report includes the selected output, category, and optional note; it does not attach another copy of the source media.
